## Phase 3 — Best Target Selection

### Winner: The Sanctuary Spa at Tradition

**Why this is the best fit**
- Positions as a **luxury** spa with 50+ treatments, packages ($160–$400+), gift certificates up to $500, and memberships ($480 / $960) — but the website looks like a mid-2000s brochure built in HTML tables.
- Primary conversion action (book appointment) is a **raw pasted Jotform URL** on the homepage. That alone is a pitchable conversion failure.
- ~20 years in business; recently expanded to ~2,500 sq ft; Google rating around **4.4 / ~324 reviews**. Established enough to write a $1k–$2k check.
- Still carries the Tradition brand name and community identity (originally Tradition Square; relocated to St. Lucie West as of late 2025). Fits the Tradition-first brief without forcing a weak Tradition-only pick.
- Not a franchise. Family-owned (Gina Grothe / Marzulli family). Decision-maker is reachable.
- One booked package or a handful of gift-card sales pays for the entire website project.

**Why $1k–$2k is appropriate**
- They need a modern brochure + booking + gift-card site, not a custom platform.
- No e-commerce complexity beyond gift vouchers/memberships they already sell.
- Competitors in medspa/spa space often look polished; this budget buys a clean, mobile-first site that closes the gap without enterprise spend.

**What the current site likely costs them**
- Lost bookings from mobile users bouncing on table layouts and a scary raw form link.
- Trust gap: "luxury spa" promise vs. dated site = higher-income Tradition / St. Lucie West residents choosing Hand & Stone, Sejour, or hotel spas instead.
- Gift-certificate friction during holidays (gift page is a dense grid of undifferentiated options).
- New location confusion — address moved; old Tradition Square listings still circulate; site messaging is patchy about the move.

**Most compelling redesign angle**
> "Your spa already feels premium in person. Your website still asks people to book through a pasted form link. I'll build a calm, modern site that matches the experience — and makes booking and gift purchases obvious on phone."

### C. Website Audit

**Visual quality**  
Fail. HTML table layout, dated header graphics, sparse imagery, no modern typography or spacing. Reads as a print flyer converted to a webpage — not a luxury spa.

**Messaging clarity**  
Mixed. Brand promise ("premier destination for relaxation") is clear, but homepage is generic wellness copy with little differentiation. Service menu is thorough on `/services.html` but presented as an endless wall of text/prices — hard to scan.

**Mobile feel**  
Likely poor. Table-based layouts and dense service lists typically break or require horizontal thinking on phones. Booking is a third-party form URL, not an embedded/mobile-optimized flow.

**Trust signals**  
Weak on-site. No review widgets despite ~300+ Google reviews. No therapist bios, before/after skin results, facility tour, or credentials beyond license numbers at footer. 20-year anniversary is mentioned but not leveraged as a trust story.

**CTA structure**  
Broken. Primary CTA is literally:

`https://form.jotform.com/250519502212142`

Gift purchase is secondary. Phone numbers compete with form link. No sticky mobile call/book bar. Membership page appears underbuilt (images with little explanatory UX).

**Conversion weaknesses**
1. Booking friction (raw external form)
2. No clear "Book now" button hierarchy
3. Gift certificates presented as a price grid without guided "perfect gift" flows
4. New location not hero-level clear on every page
5. Packages buried in long services page

**UX friction**
- Nav is flat text links in a table header
- Services page is cognitively exhausting
- About page is mostly policies (cancellation, valuables) — not brand story
- Membership page looks incomplete

**SEO / content observations**
- Brand queries likely work; competitive "spa Port St Lucie" / "massage Tradition FL" queries are probably under-optimized
- Thin homepage content; service detail exists but not structured for search (no clean service landing pages)
- Address/NAP consistency risk after relocation from Tradition Square to St. Lucie West
- Opportunity: location pages / service pages for massage, facial, couples spa, head spa, gift spa packages

### D. Pitch Angle

**Most compelling reason to upgrade now**  
They just expanded / relocated into a larger luxury space. The physical product upgraded; the digital storefront did not. Every new St. Lucie West neighbor discovering them online gets the old brochure, not the new spa.

**Best outreach angle**  
"I built a sample homepage for Sanctuary that makes booking feel as calm as your spa — no pasted form links, mobile-first, gift certificates front and center for holidays."

**Value framing for $1k–$2k**
- One couples package (~$400) or two memberships nearly covers the project
- Frame as: modern site + clearer booking + gift-card path for Q4, not a vanity redesign
- Offer: homepage + services + book + gifts + contact in this budget; membership polish as stretch

### E. Demo Strategy

**What to build**  
A calm, high-end spa marketing site demo (3–5 pages) that looks like a real replacement, not a moodboard.

**Pages / sections that matter most**
1. **Home** — brand, location clarity, Book + Buy Gift CTAs, featured packages, review strip
2. **Services** — scannable categories (Massage, Facials, Body, Nails, Specialty) with "Book" on each
3. **Packages & Gifts** — curated gift finder, not a 20-button grid
4. **About / Experience** — 20 years, expanded suite, family-owned story, facility photos
5. **Book / Contact** — clean form + phone + hours + map (St. Lucie West address)

**Improve most aggressively**
- Booking CTA (replace raw Jotform feel with polished primary button → form)
- Visual atmosphere (full-bleed serene imagery, soft motion, intentional typography)
- Mobile conversion (sticky Book / Call)
- Trust (Google review count/rating pulled into UI; policy softened into FAQ)

**Visual style that elevates them most**
- Soft spa luxury: warm stone, sage, cream, deep charcoal text — **not** purple gradients, not sterile medical white, not dark neon medspa
- Editorial serif + clean sans pairing
- Photography: treatment rooms, hands/details, couples suite, product textures — real atmosphere, not stock-only
- Motion: gentle fade-ins, soft image ken burns, CTA hover — 2–3 intentional moves max

### F. Outreach Notes

**Subject line options**
- `Built a sample site for Sanctuary Spa (booking + gifts)`

- `Quick idea for Sanctuary's new St. Lucie West location`

**Mention in outreach**

- Built a demo homepage that matches a luxury spa experience and makes Book / Gift Certificate obvious on mobile

**Why demo-worthy**
- Extreme visual gap = instant "wow"
- Clear money path (bookings + gifts + memberships)
- Owner-operated, not franchise-locked
